Mukhija Skin & Laser Clinic has been a recognised dermatology, cosmetic and laser centre in Gorakhpur for decades. Dr. R. D. Advanced Treatment Technology

The equipment listed on our current site and used across treatments at the clinic.

AcuPulse CO2 Laser

Fractional CO2 laser used across acne scar, open pore and skin resurfacing treatments.

MedLite C6 Laser

Q-switched Nd:YAG laser used for pigment, acne marks and open pore treatment.

LightSheer Desire Diode
